# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary

Engineering Managers lead technical execution, team health, and delivery quality. They translate product priorities into achievable engineering plans while supporting team performance, capacity, and cross-functional coordination.

### Responsibilities

- Own technical execution and delivery predictability for the team

- Align staffing, roadmap, and sprint commitments with project needs

- Support engineering quality, sustainability, and mentorship

- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and capacity constraints

- Partner with Product and Project leads to balance priorities and trade-offs

### Goals

- Deliver reliable software on schedule with strong engineering quality

- Maintain healthy team performance and sustainable delivery pace

- Improve planning accuracy and reduce avoidable blockers

### Typical Communication

- Weekly delivery planning and team health discussions

- Sprint reviews, planning sessions, and engineering dependency reviews

- Cross-functional alignment with PMs and Product Managers

### Interaction with Existing Roles

- Works with Project Managers on milestones, capacity, and risk tracking

- Collaborates with Product Managers on scope, sequencing, and trade-offs

- Supports Developers by clarifying priorities and removing delivery friction

- Coordinates with QA Leads on quality gates and release readiness

---

## Quality / QA Leads

### Role Summary

Quality / QA Leads define the testing strategy, validation approach, and release quality bar. They ensure that product changes meet acceptance criteria and are safe to release.

### Responsibilities

- Define test coverage strategy and quality standards

- Validate features against acceptance criteria and business goals

- Coordinate manual and automated QA activities

- Identify defects, regressions, and risk areas early

- Partner with release teams to confirm production readiness

### Goals

- Reduce defects and escapes to production

- Clarify what “done” means for quality and release

- Improve confidence in deployment decisions

### Typical Communication

- Daily or sprint-level QA readiness checks

- Defect triage, release gates, and regression reviews

- Collaboration with Developers and Product stakeholders on acceptance validation

### Interaction with Existing Roles

- Works with Developers to clarify expectations and testability

- Supports Project Managers by providing readiness signals for milestones and releases

- Partners with Product Managers on user-facing quality and acceptance criteria

- Coordinates with Security / Compliance Partners for risk assessments and compliance validation

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary

Security / Compliance Partners ensure that projects meet required security, privacy, and regulatory standards. They help teams identify risks early and embed safeguards into delivery work.

### Responsibilities

- Review security and compliance requirements for the project

- Assess risk for data handling, integrations, and technical design choices

- Recommend secure patterns and controls

- Support audits, reviews, and incident response preparation

- Help define escalation paths for high-risk issues

### Goals

- Reduce exposure to security and compliance risk

- Build trust in systems and release decisions

- Ensure risk is addressed early rather than late in delivery

### Typical Communication

- Design reviews, dependency assessments, and release approvals

- Risk and compliance briefings with PM and Product leadership

- Security issue tracking and escalation coordination

### Interaction with Existing Roles

- Advises Product Managers on risk trade-offs and compliance constraints

- Supports Project Managers with risk mitigation planning and escalation paths

- Works with Developers and Engineering Managers to embed secure practices in delivery

- Coordinates with QA Leads on vulnerability testing and release checks

---

## Customer / Stakeholder Success Representatives

### Role Summary

Customer / Stakeholder Success Representatives capture user needs, stakeholder expectations, and operational realities. They help connect customer value to delivery work and sharpen product decisions.

### Responsibilities

- Collect stakeholder input, feedback, and adoption concerns

- Translate customer needs into product or delivery priorities

- Represent operational and user experience realities in planning

- Support launch readiness and stakeholder communication

- Identify risk areas tied to adoption, satisfaction, or business impact

### Goals

- Improve customer value realization and stakeholder confidence

- Ensure decisions reflect end-user needs and delivery realities

- Strengthen alignment between business priorities and implementation

### Typical Communication

- Stakeholder briefings, user feedback loops, and milestone updates

- Coordination with Product Managers and PMs on communications and expectations

- Support discussions tied to adoption, release impact, and early feedback

### Interaction with Existing Roles

- Partners with Product Managers to validate priorities and success metrics

- Supports Project Managers by surfacing stakeholder concerns and communication needs

- Provides Developers with context on user pain points and expected outcomes

- Helps Sponsors and leadership understand business impact and adoption risk

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary

Support / Operations Representatives ensure that new features and services are operationally viable. They focus on readiness, monitoring, supportability, and sustainable production workflows.

### Responsibilities

- Identify operational readiness requirements for features and services

- Define monitoring, alerting, and escalation expectations

- Help assess support burden and enablement needs

- Support incident readiness and handoff to production operations

- Partner with engineering and release teams on post-deployment validation

### Goals

- Reduce operational friction and service disruption

- Improve incident response readiness and user support outcomes

- Ensure the business can sustain and support the solution post-release

### Typical Communication

- Release readiness reviews, operational handoff discussions, and support planning

- Incident response coordination and post-release monitoring reviews

- Collaboration with PMs, engineering, and release stakeholders

### Interaction with Existing Roles

- Works with Project Managers on launch planning and readiness checklists

- Supports Release teams with cutover planning and operational validation

- Coordinates with Developers and Engineering Managers on monitoring and supportability

- Provides insight to Product Managers about customer support impact and service health

---

## Sponsors / Executive Sponsors

### Role Summary

Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding alignment, and executive support. They ensure the project remains aligned to broader business outcomes and can escalate decisions when needed.

### Responsibilities

- Sponsor vision, investment, and priority alignment

- Help resolve cross-functional trade-offs and strategic roadblocks

- Support business case validation and longer-term value realization

- Provide executive escalation pathways for high-impact decisions

- Reinforce accountability for outcomes and key milestones

### Goals

- Ensure strategic alignment and organizational support

- Reduce uncertainty in major project decisions

- Enable successful delivery of high-value outcomes

### Typical Communication

- Executive checkpoints, portfolio updates, and milestone reviews

- Decision support during escalations and major trade-offs

- Business impact and value communications with PM and Product leadership

### Interaction with Existing Roles

- Works with Product Managers on strategic prioritization and business value

- Supports Project Managers in resolving escalations and resource constraints

- Partners with Engineering Managers and Security / Compliance Partners on risk and delivery trade-offs

- Provides oversight for executive alignment and sponsor-level communication

---

## How these additional roles strengthen the OctoAcme model

These roles do not replace the existing personas; instead, they extend the delivery model to make accountability and collaboration more explicit across the lifecycle of a project. By defining how work flows between Product, Engineering, Project, Quality, Security, Customer, Support, and Executive stakeholders, the team can make faster decisions, reduce ambiguity, and improve execution quality.

The goal is not to add bureaucracy, but to create a clearer map of ownership:

- Product defines the why and the outcome

- Project Management coordinates the how and the timeline

- Engineering delivers the solution

- QA validates readiness and quality

- Security and Compliance keep risk in check

- Customer and Support perspectives keep the work grounded in real-world impact

- Sponsors ensure strategic alignment and executive support

This broader role model improves clarity for onboarding, reduces handoff gaps, and supports stronger outcomes across planning, execution, release, and continuous improvement.
